Alexander (Sasha) Apartsin, Ph.D.

Senior Lecturer, School of Computer Science, Faculty of Sciences · Holon Institute of Technology (HIT)

Machine-learning-systems researcher with two decades of building and measuring AI at production scale, now working at the intersection of energy-efficient AI computation, measurement methodology, and optimization. Principal investigator for the JouleFlex proposal on energy-optimal power control for AI data centers.

Fit to an energy-and-AI research program. Three threads of this record converge on the proposed work: (1) a peer-reviewed lineage in energy-efficient signal estimation and measurement under noise, including an IEEE JSTARS paper on energy-efficient time-of-flight estimation; (2) a sustained output of benchmark and measurement methodologies for AI systems, the exact skill an energy-per-useful-compute metric requires; (3) senior industry roles carrying deployment and techno-economic responsibility (AVP at Citi, Head of AI at Motorola Solutions, Head of Data Science at Questar), where compute cost and capacity modeling were operational concerns.

Current research direction

Energy-optimal operation of AI accelerators: measuring where energy per unit of useful compute is minimized on GPUs (the "Joule Point"), modeling the underlying power–time tradeoff, and turning it into fleet-design, control, and grid-facing decisions. The foundational measurement study is a manuscript under review; the JouleFlex proposal to the Israeli Ministry of Energy extends it into an open dataset, prediction models, and a decision-support toolkit for energy-constrained AI data centers.
