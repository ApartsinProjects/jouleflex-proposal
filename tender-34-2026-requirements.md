# קול קורא 34/2026 — binding requirements digest

Source: <https://www.gov.il/he/pages/tender-34-2026> · PDF: [tender-34-2026.pdf](../docs/tender-34-2026.pdf) (76 pp, published 11.08.2026, updated 12.08.2026)
Topics reference: [rd-support-2026.pdf](../docs/rd-support-2026.pdf) (מדיניות המו"פ 2026)

## 1. Scoring (טבלה 1, p.7) — total 100

| # | Criterion | Max |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | מצוינות מדעית (scientific excellence: goals, clarity, credibility of approach) | 10 |
| 2 | חדשנות (originality vs. knowledge in Israel and worldwide) | 15 |
| 3 | מידת ההתאמה ליעדי המו"פ של המשרד (fit to the Ministry's stated R&D goals) | 15 |
| 4 | תכנית העבודה (work plan: methods, feasibility, risk handling) | 17 |
| 5 | אנשי הצוות (team experience and fit of expertise) | 10 |
| 6 | **התועלת וההשפעה** (benefit and impact, incl. training of manpower and students) | **20** |
| 7 | תקציב (budget realism and balance vs. work plan) | 8 |
| 8 | טיב הבקשה (presentation, completeness) | 5 |

Each criterion is graded in discrete bands: 100 / 90 / 80 / 70 / 60 / 50 / 40 / 0 percent of its max.

**Threshold (§5.8): ≥75 total AND ≥60% in every single criterion.** One criterion at 50% disqualifies regardless of total.

**Bonus (§5.7): up to +5%** of the overall score for academia-industry collaboration, inter-institutional collaboration, and international collaboration. Judged on partner quality and added skills/resources.

**Ranking lists (§6.1-6.3):** a central list receives 50% of the budget; the rest goes to dedicated per-domain lists. Assignment follows the domain and topic **the proposer selects**. A score ≥75 can still lose if that list's budget is exhausted (§6.6).

Other process notes: no budget existed at publication, awards are conditional on funding arriving (p.1). A proposal rejected twice consecutively without material changes may be disqualified (§5.5); resubmitters must state the changes explicitly (§5.6). The Ministry may use an anonymous referee (רפרנט) (§5.4).

## 2. Duration and start

- Work plan period: **up to 2 years** from contract signature (נספח א'1 §1.3).
- Work may start **no earlier than 1.1.2027** (§1.4).

## 3. Budget (נספח טז')

| Duration | Maximum approved |
|---|---|
| 1 year | ₪300,000 |
| 2 to 3 years | ₪500,000, **and no more than ₪250,000 per year** |

- **Overhead (תקורה) ≤ 15%**, sits **inside** the requested total, computed on actual costs, excluding subcontractors (§7.1). Costs covered by overhead may not be itemised separately (§7.3).
- **Personnel ≤ 60%** of the annual budget; up to 80% only with reasoned justification accepted by the sub-committee (§2.4).
- **No salary for tenured / budgeted (ות"ת) faculty** (§1.1). The PI serves **without salary** (§2.6).
- Scholarship ceilings, full-time-equivalent per year: MSc ₪120k, PhD ₪150k, postdoc ₪168k; non-faculty researcher/engineer/technician ≤ ₪360k (§2.3).
- **Equipment ≤ 20% of the annual budget** (§4.4). **Computing equipment is reimbursed at only 33% of its value per research year** (§4.5). Equipment purchased before project start is not recognised (§4.2). Dedicated single-purpose equipment can reach 100% with prior approval plus a detailed justification letter (§4.6).
- Subcontractors and external work go in their own section, with detailed quotes (§6).
- Conferences, professional publication, **dedicated software licences**, courses, patent costs: allowed under שונות (§5.2). Publication ≤ ₪10,000 per article (§5.4.9).
- Foreign travel: economy only, ≤ ₪8,000 return; per diem ≤ ₪250/day; lodging ≤ ₪650/day. Students reimbursed at 75% (100% in international collaborations) (§5.4).
- **The budget spec must be written in Hebrew** (§1.3). No double funding from any government source (§1.5.2, §2.10). A CPA-signed salary table is required (§2.2).

## 4. Format (נספח א'1 §2)

- Language of the description: **Hebrew or English** (§2.1).
- Font **David 12pt**, **1.5 line spacing**, margins **≥2.54 cm** all round (§2.2).
- Sections **2.5.4 through 2.5.9 must not exceed 10 pages**. Graphs, tables and diagrams may go in appendices of **≤5 pages**, A4 (§2.2). Deviation may affect ranking (§2.3).
- **Abstract ≤300 words, required in BOTH Hebrew and English** (§2.5.2), entered in the online form and included in the description document. The abstract is public and may be published by the Ministry, so it must contain no confidential material.
- §2.4 explicitly recommends addressing the Table 1 criteria directly in the proposal. Write to the rubric.

### Required sections, in order

| § | Section |
|---|---|
| 2.5.1 | Title, researchers, institution |
| 2.5.2 | Abstract (Hebrew + English, ≤300 words) |
| 2.5.3 | Glossary of abbreviations |
| 2.5.4 | Objectives: the research question or problem |
| 2.5.5 | Proposed research: scientific/technological background; review of existing knowledge; **knowledge gaps**; description; methods; novelty; **fit to Ministry R&D goals**; **preliminary results** |
| 2.5.6 | Application: expected benefit and impact (techno-economic, environmental or social) |
| 2.5.7 | Work plan: stages, deliverables, duration, success metrics, **budget per stage**, risks, mitigations |
| 2.5.8 | The applicant: researchers, project manager, partners, available resources |
| 2.5.9 | Funding: all other actual and potential funding sources |
| 2.5.10 | Bibliography |
| §3 | Gantt (filled in the online system): milestones, timing, quantitative or judgeable deliverables |
